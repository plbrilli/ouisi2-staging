How to play

  1. Step 1

    Find it

    Every day brings a new Photo Card. Look around for something that looks like it, then take a photo – or choose one you've already taken.

  2. Step 2

    Your pair

    Your photo sits beside the day's Photo Card. That's your pair: save it to your photos, or send it to anyone.

  3. Step 3

    Past Dailies

    The last 11 days are kept in Past Dailies. After that they're gone, so save the ones you love.

Privacy

Your photos stay on your phone.

There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to log into. Your pictures go nowhere unless you share them, and we collect no information about you at all.

What's OuiSi?

Pronounced "We-See," OuiSi translates to "Yes Yes" from other languages. It's a nod to our belief that pictures are a language nearly all of us speak. OuiSi is based in the NY Hudson Valley.

OuiSi Photologue

Play with the people you love, wherever they are. You send a photo, they answer with one that looks like it, and back and forth it goes.

We're testing it by invitation for now.

How to play

  1. Step 1

    Start a Photologue

    Each game is called a Photologue. Sign in with your Apple ID, pick the name your friends will see, and invite a friend – they join with the link you send.

  2. Step 2

    Answer with a photo

    Take a photo of something that looks like theirs – in shape, color or pattern – and send it. They answer yours the same way.

  3. Step 3

    Say what you saw

    Comment on any pair to tell each other what you saw – or just keep the photos coming.

Privacy

Who sees your photos

Only the people in a Photologue see its photos. They're kept with Apple, in a database that belongs to OuiSi, and we open it only to keep the app running. No ads, no trackers, and we never sell anything.

What's OuiSi?

Pronounced "We-See," OuiSi translates to "Yes Yes" from other languages. It's a nod to our belief that pictures are a language nearly all of us speak. OuiSi is based in the NY Hudson Valley.

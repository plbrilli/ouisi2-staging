Privacy Policy — OuiSi Daily

Effective September 1, 2026

This policy covers the OuiSi Daily iPhone app, made by OuiSi Inc. (Hudson Valley, New York).

The short version: we collect nothing. Not a little — nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, and no way for us to know you are using the app.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no list to put here.

Your photo stays on your phone

Each day the app shows one photo, the same one for everyone. The photo you add in response is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded, it is excluded from iCloud Backup, and it is deleted automatically once it is more than 11 days old. We never receive it and could not retrieve it if asked.

If you want to keep one, share or save the image — that is the only copy that survives.

The one thing the app does over the internet

It downloads the day's photo from playouisi.com. That request tells our web host the same thing any website visit does: that a device asked for a file. We don't log it, analyse it, or connect it to anything.

Camera and photo library

Used only at the moment you choose to take or pick a photo. The app receives the single image you select and nothing else, and never browses your library.

Your signature

If you add a signature, it is stored on your device and drawn into images you choose to share. It is never sent to us.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no identifiers
  • No analytics, no tracking SDKs, no advertising
  • No third-party code of any kind
  • No selling or sharing data, because there is none to sell or share

Children

OuiSi Daily collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: reach us through the contact page at ouisi.co.

What's OuiSi?

Pronounced "We-See," OuiSi translates to "Yes Yes" from other languages. It's a nod to our belief that pictures are a language nearly all of us speak. OuiSi is based in the NY Hudson Valley.
